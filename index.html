Bio

With over a decade of experience in research and developing data analytics platforms, Rana Alotaibi is currently co-founding a startup operating in stealth mode and is an Assistant Professor at KACST. Previously, she was a Senior Scientist at Microsoft Gray Systems Lab (GSL) in Redmond, WA, where she focused on query optimization within Microsoft Fabric's unified Lakehouse ecosystem to accelerate cloud analytics. She began her career at IBM Almaden Research Center in San Jose, CA, working on query optimization for hybrid data stores.

Alotaibi earned her Ph.D. and M.Sc. in Computer Science from UC San Diego, specializing in semantic query optimization for data systems. Her research has been published in leading conferences, including SIGMOD, VLDB, ICDE, and CIDR. She holds five U.S. patents and, together with a distinguished team of data systems scientists, received the Microsoft Patent Award in both 2023 and 2024. She also actively contributes to the research community by serving on the program committees of leading data systems conferences, including SIGMOD and VLDB.

Research

Research focuses on query and workload optimization, autonomous data services, and data systems storage.
